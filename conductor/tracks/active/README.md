# Active

Tracks currently in progress. At most one should be `active` at a time — parallel active
tracks means two half-built systems and no decision point.

## Contents

**No active track.** Nothing is currently in progress.

## Entry criteria

A track moves here when its spec and plan exist and it is the agreed next unit of work.

## Exit criteria

Complete all plan tasks, hit every acceptance criterion in the spec, then move to `closed/`.
If the track turns out to rest on a wrong assumption, record the finding and move it to
`backlog/` or `closed/` rather than leaving it abandoned.
